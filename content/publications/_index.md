## Research in progress

### Energy Efficiency and Power Management in Serverless Computing

My current PhD research investigates the relationship between application performance, resource utilisation, and power consumption in serverless computing environments.

The work explores software-based mechanisms for monitoring and controlling power consumption while maintaining application performance.
