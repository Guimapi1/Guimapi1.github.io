## Thesis

**Energy Efficiency of Applications in the Cloud**\
PhD in Computer Science at IMT Atlantique (LS2N, STACK team), Nantes — since January 2025.\
Supervised by [Jean-Marc Menaud](http://menaud.fr/) and [Remous-Aris Koutsiamanis](https://ariskou.com/).

The goal of the thesis is to reduce the energy consumption of cloud applications, using software-level mechanisms that the platform itself can apply.

[More about the thesis →]({{< relref "phd-research" >}})

## Current focus

I currently work on **serverless platforms**, where I study how to enforce a strict energy budget on function executions without breaking the consistency of the applications that run on them. The system is implemented on Apache OpenWhisk and evaluated on the Grid'5000 testbed; an article is in preparation.

## Interests

Energy-efficient computing · Serverless computing · Energy budgets and power management · Scheduling and resource management · Experimental evaluation on real testbeds
